# Act 2 — a third family

Thornbury adds **wide-format** presses: `WideFormatPlate` ("wide-format
vinyl plate"), `WideFormatInkSystem` ("wide-format solvent-ink
**fountain**"), and `WideFormatFeeder` ("wide-format roll feeder").

Watch the ink system's name: offset's is also a "fountain" (`"offset
wet-ink fountain"`). The two are unrelated parts of unrelated families
that happen to share a word - not a hint to reuse `OffsetInkSystem`,
and not related to it at all.

`buildPress("wide-format")` has to work, producing a matched
wide-format press, exactly the way `"digital"` and `"offset"` already do.
